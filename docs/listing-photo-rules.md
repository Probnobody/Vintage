# Listing photo and posting rules

## Photos

- Use at least three distinct photos of the actual item on every live listing. Start with a front or main view, add the back or another angle, and include a label, maker’s mark, or useful detail. Add views for closures, construction, and every visible flaw as needed.
- Shoot square-on and level. Center the item, keep spacing even, and straighten tilt and perspective where it preserves the real shape. Keep naturally asymmetric details. Use a better angle or reshoot if correction would stretch or crop the item.
- Keep fingers out of the frame when shooting. Crop or remove a stray finger only if the edit preserves the item’s edges, shape, texture, and condition. Use another photo or reshoot if the finger covers the item and removal would require guessing.
- Reduce glare with soft light, a slight change in camera or light angle, or a better source photo. Retouch remaining glare only where the edit preserves color, finish, detail, and flaws. Reshoot when reflections obscure something a buyer needs to see.
- Use Codex/ChatGPT or eBay’s background remover when a cutout helps. Photoroom is optional if available. Check the finished edges against the original, especially on lace, straps, transparent objects, and white items. Keep detail, label, and flaw photos as shot apart from basic cropping or leveling.

## Measurements and posting

- Measure directly when the item is in hand. When working from photos, compare views to locate endpoints and cross-check readings. Use a readable tape, ruler, or known scale in the same plane as the item. Record units and how the measurement was taken, such as flat pit to pit or framed outside dimensions.
- Label credible photo-derived measurements as approximate. Keep guesses, conflicting readings, and measurements without a reliable scale in the review notes for a physical recheck. Keep a listing in draft if an essential measurement is unresolved.
- Before posting, check the photo count and order, condition and flaw disclosures, measurements, price, and shipping. On eBay, use the item’s Drive folder and check the mobile preview. Keep internal review notes with the item, outside the buyer-facing description.

## After posting

Leave Ricky a short private write-up for each listing with the item or SKU, marketplace, and live link. Note which photos were edited and any remaining glare, obscured detail, or angle that needs review. List measurements to recheck, with the source photo and reason for uncertainty. Flag any other specific details Ricky should verify on the live page, such as photo order or crop, condition, price, or shipping. If there are no outstanding checks, say so.
