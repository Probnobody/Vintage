**How the numbers were made.** Each piece lies on the table with a sewing tape (inch side) beside it. The tape is not laid across each dimension, so every figure is computed: pixel points on the garment, scaled by 16 to 32 inch marks on the tape, corrected for camera tilt. Tape fits were good to about 0.05 in. Rounded to the half inch; treat the plain figures as good to about half an inch. The tape runs one way only, so tilt the other way could add up to about 3% on some lengths.

**Figures marked (?) are not safe to publish.** The piece was not lying spread flat for that dimension. Put the tape across it before listing, or leave the line out. Nothing was nudged toward a size chart.
